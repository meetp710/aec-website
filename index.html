Precision since 1977

Allied Engineering Company was founded in Ahmedabad by Mr. G. H. Panchal in 1977. For nearly five decades we have machined precision components for manufacturers across India.

Whatever the size of the job, we bring the right machine, people who know it well, and quality checks through the whole production cycle. That is why most of our work comes from clients who keep coming back.

Our capabilities

We machine castings, forgings and fabricated structures into finished parts, and take on job work using your own material.

Single prototypes or repeat batches, every part is checked before it leaves our plant.

Machined Components

Finished parts machined to your drawing and ready to assemble. Shafts, bushes, gears, flanges and housings are milled, bored, turned and drilled under one roof.

Fewer hand-offs between shops means tighter control on every critical feature.

Castings & Forgings

We take rough castings and forged blanks and machine the faces, bores and holes that matter. Large rings, discs and flanges run on our vertical turret lathe.

Heavy housings and bases go on the floor borer and plano millers for true, square surfaces.

Enquire Now

Fabrication & Job Work

Welded frames and structures get machined pads, bores and mounting faces after fabrication so they sit level and line up with what bolts onto them.

Need extra capacity? Send your material and drawing and we machine it on our machines with our operators.

A machining powerhouse

Two plants in Ahmedabad run a mix of CNC and heavy conventional machine tools. Vertical and horizontal machining centres, a floor borer, single and double column plano millers, a radial drill and a vertical turret lathe.

Together they handle small precise parts as well as large, heavy components that many shops cannot hold.
